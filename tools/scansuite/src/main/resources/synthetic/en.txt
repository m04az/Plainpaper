Plainpaper turns photos of paper into documents that look like they came from a flatbed scanner.
Every step runs on the phone itself: nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and there are no watermarks.
The engine finds the edges of the page, straightens it into a rectangle, removes shadows and colour casts, and renders the text crisp and dark on pure white paper.
Receipts, contracts, identity cards and handwritten notes all follow the same path, in Arabic, English or French.
Quality is measured, not guessed: a fixed set of reference photos is processed on every change and compared against thresholds.
When a page is curled, the text lines are straightened before the final rendering so that each baseline stays horizontal.
The output is produced at three hundred dots per inch on an A4 or Letter sized page, ready to share as a PDF.
